Privacy Policy

Visiting Judges Case Manager · Effective September 11, 2026

The short version: the app sends nothing to us, ever. Your cases, documents, time, expenses, and statements stay on your device. They leave it only when you send, share, or save them yourself.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you put into Visiting Judges Case Manager is stored in the app’s own storage on your iPhone or iPad: your profile, each assignment and its order, the documents you add, hearings and travel, pending items, notes, time and expense entries, and your statements and their status. That includes information about other people that a case naturally carries, such as the names of parties and the names and email addresses of counsel and clerks. The app has no server of ours and no account system, and it does not send your records anywhere on its own.

Deleting the app deletes this data from the device. If you back up your device, the backup may include the app’s data like any other app’s. Device backups are managed by Apple under Apple’s terms, and we never see them.

When your information leaves the device

Only when you tell it to, and only to the place you choose:

  • Email. When you email a statement or write to counsel, the app opens a draft in your device’s Mail app. You review it and you send it; it goes through your own email account.
  • Sharing and printing. A statement you save as a PDF, print, or share goes where you send it.
  • Your calendar. If you choose a calendar in Settings, the app writes the hearings and travel days you add to it. If that calendar syncs with an online service, such as Google Calendar or iCloud, that service carries those events under its own terms.
  • Backups. A backup is one file that you save wherever you choose, such as Files, iCloud Drive, or Google Drive. It stays under your control.

What the app does not do

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, and no third-party data-collection software. It does not read your contacts or your location. It uses the camera or photo library only when you choose to photograph or attach a document, through the system’s own picker. The Face ID or passcode lock is performed by iOS: the app learns only whether you unlocked it, and never sees your face data or your passcode.

In the App Store’s privacy label, this is Data Not Collected.

Subscriptions

Your subscription is sold, billed, and managed by Apple (or, on Android, by Google Play). We receive no payment details, no card numbers, and no personal information from your purchase. The app asks the store on your device whether a subscription is active, and nothing more. Store sales reports give us totals, not the names of subscribers.

Court records are yours to protect

The app keeps your records on your device precisely because they are court records. You remain responsible for handling them as the law and your court’s rules require: for example, keeping the device locked, deciding what to back up and where, and taking care with what you email or share.

Children

The app is made for judges and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a future version ever changes any of the above, for example by adding sync between your devices, this policy will be updated first, with its effective date revised and the change described plainly.
