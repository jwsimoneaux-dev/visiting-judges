Everything a visiting judge keeps

Built around how assignments actually run: an order from a presiding judge, a courthouse a few hours away, and a statement that has to be right.

The order of assignment

The cause, the court, the judge you sit for, the scope, and the authority. Limited-scope orders are flagged everywhere the case appears.

Documents

Import PDFs or photograph a page. Files are named by date and title, the way chambers files them, and read right in the app.

Hearings and travel

A month calendar with travel days, lodging, and the round trip, sent to the calendar you already use. A final order that must be signed in the county seat is flagged on the hearing.

Pending

What is waiting on you, by due date, across every case, with overdue items flagged.

Time

Full and half days where Chapter 74 counts days; actual hours, in person and remote, where the statutory probate courts ask for hours.

Expenses

Mileage from your own starting point at the rate in effect on the date of travel, with meals, lodging, and the per diem, and a receipt for each.

Statements that cite the right statute

Choose the kind of assignment once. The app counts your time the way that statute does, names who pays what, and cites the subsection behind each line. Tap any ⓘ in the app for the rule in plain words.

Addressed to the right office

All eleven regional presiding judges and the presiding judge of the statutory probate courts are built in, with their administrators’ addresses and email, or type in a county judge.

Never claimed twice

Submitting a statement locks its entries, and the next one starts fresh. Track each from submitted to forwarded to paid.

Receipts attached

Print, save a PDF, or email the statement with its receipts numbered as exhibits.

Private by design

Your cases never leave your iPhone or iPad unless you send them. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no tracking. Face ID or a passcode guards the app, and a single backup file, saved wherever you choose, carries everything to a new device. Read the privacy policy.
